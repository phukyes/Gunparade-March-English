Gunparade March - English Translation
Playtest build GPM-EN61
Translated by Phukyes/Asterhex
Discord: https://discord.gg/xcyEFG4YG8

This is not an ai translation. No ai was used in the creation of this patch.

this is a playtest build. starting with EN60 the patch only includes lines that have been
translated and edited, so the game is no longer fully playable in English. right now that covers
the opening hours of the game (about 1,600 lines). menus are in English. the rest of the story and
small talk, plus item names and descriptions, is still in Japanese and will be filled in as the
work goes on. new builds every couple of days.
if you encounter any bugs in the English sections please reach out to me via discord with a
screenshot and the current build of the patch.

KNOWN BUGS IN THE ORIGINAL GAME
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
the original game shipped with some progress breaking bugs. they are in the Japanese release
too, so they are not caused by the patch. if you hit one of these please dont report it as a patch bug, but do reach out if you are not sure.

1. The game freezes as the Battle of Kumamoto Castle starts.
   Happens if you are in any department other than Pilot.
   Switch to Pilot before that battle comes up.

2. Your unit stops taking commands in battle and you get a game over.
   Can happen in long battles with the battle demos on.
   Turn battle demos OFF in the settings.

3. Kumamoto Castle happens before your first sortie. characters
   look grey and the battle is brutal.
   Don't push Mai's events forward until the whole platoon has joined.

4. Things break after an airborne drop battle (your character "ghosts").
   Happens if you are the Unit 3 pilot with no partner.
   Turn down the Brigadier General's offer to join the drop.

5. The game freezes in the event where Takigawa explains
   relationships.
   Happens if that event fires right after you change your Shikon's
   equipment. after an equipment change, stay away from Takigawa until
   that event has played.

6. The game freezes on the strategy meeting screen.
   Happens if a classmate's proposal passes before the whole platoon has
   joined. vote those proposals down until everyone is there.

7. The game freezes on the strategy meeting screen.
   Happens if the class president or vice-president is suspended and
   cant attend. dont hold meetings while either one is suspended.

save often, and keep more than one memory card save just in case.
